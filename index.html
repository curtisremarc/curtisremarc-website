Curtis Remarc began his career over a decade ago on the open format dance floors of Atlantic City casino clubs. Since then his wide array of selection and skills have brought him around the world to play from NYC to Tokyo. But being a mainstay of Philadelphia and NYC area clubs isn’t all he is known for. He has signed music to Nervous and Stealth Records, garnered praise from the likes of Mark Farina and Fatboy Slim, had music aired on BBC Radio 1, and done events for hundreds of organizations big and small. Wherever the party and whatever the music, CURTIS WILL BRING THE HEAT.
